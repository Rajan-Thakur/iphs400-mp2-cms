About

Computer Science is fun

Our goal is to make Computer Science an exciting opportunity for you to pursue and learn more about in a fun way, in a world where computer technology and software continuously evolves.

Why CS matters a lot

As you may have noticed, Artificial Intelligence is developing rapidly, and believe it or not, AI is a part of Computer Science! Studying AI and how to work with it is essential for the future. And AI won't necessarily take jobs but it will rather rotate and transform them, so having the expertise in this field will really be helpful in the future.

Why should you join?

Whether you're a die-hard intended CS major for college or you barely know how to code, you can still join if you're eligible because the events are fun and the food is good and free.

Affiliation

Our CSHS at the high school is affiliated with the national Computer Science Teachers Association (CSTA).

Eligibility

In order to join, you must satisfy three criteria:

  • Have a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or above
  • Have completed or be enrolled in two or more CS courses at the high school.
  • Submit an application by the October deadline

Requirements & Awards

  • If you'll be joining as a Senior, you must complete 20 hours of community service throughout the year if you want to get a light blue honor cord and a blue CSHS pin for graduation
  • If you'll be joining not as a Senior, you'll need to complete 10 hours of community service.

Questions

  • If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us.
